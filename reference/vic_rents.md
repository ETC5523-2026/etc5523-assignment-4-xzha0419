# Median rent of VIC by local government area of each quarter

Median rent of VIC by local government area of each quarter

## Usage

``` r
vic_rents
```

## Format

A data frame with 58618 rows and 6 column

- quarter:

  First day of each quarter with median rent

- property_type:

  Property type of the property from 1 bedroom to 4 bedroom house and
  all properties

- lga:

  Local government area

- region:

  Departmental region the area belongs to

- Median:

  Median weekly rent in Australian dollars

- Count:

  Number of new lettings the median is based on

## Source

Homes Victoria, Rental Report - Quarterly: Quarterly Median Rents by
LGA, September quarter 2025. Licensed CC BY 3.0 AU.
<https://data.gov.au/data/dataset/rental-report-quarterly-quarterly-median-rents-by-lga>

## Details

Median only cover the new rental price but not all property resources.
Homes Victoria will hide the data and show in NA if the quarter record
only have too few trades.
